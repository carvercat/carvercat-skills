# Logo animation

A logo animation (sting, reveal, resolve) brings a mark to life in a few seconds and ends on the logo
exactly as the brand guide draws it. It is short, repeated often, and seen by everyone, so it has to be
simple, memorable and on-brand.

## The idea

- **Build from the logo's own parts**: strokes draw, shapes assemble, letters arrive, a counter-form
  reveals. The motion should look like it came from the mark, not from a preset.
- **Personality from the brand**: a bank resolves calmly; a toy brand bounces; a tech brand clicks into
  place; a luxury house fades and tracks.
- **End on the master artwork**: the last frames show the logo at its correct proportions, colours and
  clear space, held long enough to register.
- **Sound logo**: a short sound tied to the key moment (lock, snap, chime) doubles recall.

## Rules of thumb

| Use | Length |
|---|---|
| Social outro | 2–4 s |
| Web, app splash | 2–5 s (app splashes shorter) |
| Video intro or ident | 3–6 s |
| Cinematic reveal | 5–10 s |

- Start at 3–4 s and lengthen only if the motion needs it; viewers wait for the content after it.
- One hero move (a stamp, a draw-on, an assembly), then a short settle, then the hold.
- Hold the final lock-up at least 1–2 s.
- Make three versions: full, short (about 2 s) and a static end frame.

## Do and don't

- Do read the brand guide (clear space, minimum size, colour on dark/light) before animating.
- Do keep shapes simple and the move readable at small sizes.
- Don't distort the logo at rest (wrong proportions, rotated, recoloured).
- Don't fly logo pieces in from random places; pop or assemble them in place.

## Checklist

- [ ] Final frames match the master artwork exactly.
- [ ] Motion personality matches the brand.
- [ ] Short and long versions, plus a still.
- [ ] Sound hit on the key frame.
