# Accessibility

Motion that everyone can watch safely and read comfortably. Two of these pages carry measured limits of
Carvercat's own checks; use them on every delivery.

| Page | One line | Measured in Carvercat |
|---|---|---|
| [Photosensitivity and flashing](photosensitivity.md) | No more than 3 flashes a second over a quarter of the frame | qc passes 3/s, flags 3.75/s; ignores 6.25% areas; conservative on dark flicker |
| [Motion sensitivity](motion-sensitivity.md) | Zooms, spins and parallax that make people ill | lint and qc did not flag a 3× zoom in 10 f with two spins: judge by eye |
| [Readability](readability.md) | Size, contrast, position, length, time | lint: 18 px line height (Rethink Sans 0.725 × size), contrast, safe box, 17 cps |
| [Captions and subtitles](captions.md) | Sound-off viewing and accessible speech | theory (lint rules in reading time) |
| [Colour vision deficiency](colour-vision.md) | Never colour alone | theory |
