# Glitch

A hub page: what the glitch style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Edgy and digital: RGB splits, slice shifts, noise bars and stepped jumps, in short controlled bursts.

## Principles that carry it

- [Straight ahead and pose to pose](../principles/straight-ahead-and-pose-to-pose.md): stepped Hold-key jumps.
- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 8–12 f bursts.
- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): glitches on beats, quiet in between.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): slice shifts with masks.
- [Rhythm editing (cutting to music)](../techniques/rhythm-editing.md): glitch cuts on the beat.
- [Seamless loops](../techniques/looping.md): noise overlays.

## Where it is used

- [Social ads and short-form](../genres/social-ads.md)
- [Lyric videos](../genres/lyric-videos.md)
- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)

## Roots

- [Broadcast graphics of the 1980s and 1990s](../history/broadcast-graphics-80s-90s.md)

## Watch out

- Photosensitivity first: run `qc` on every glitch moment; keep bursts under 0.5 s and areas small.
- Text must be readable once the glitch settles.
- Use glitch as accent, not texture for the whole film.
