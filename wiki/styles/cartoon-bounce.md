# Cartoon and bouncy

A hub page: what the cartoon and bouncy style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Elastic, warm and character-driven. Squash and stretch, anticipation, springs and real contact with the ground.

## Principles that carry it

- [Squash and stretch](../principles/squash-and-stretch.md): 115/85 to 125/75 on contact, volume kept.
- [Anticipation](../principles/anticipation.md): crouch before every jump.
- [Arcs](../principles/arcs.md): jumps on parabolas, swings on pivots.
- [Follow-through and overlapping action](../principles/follow-through-and-overlap.md): parts settle after the body.
- [Secondary action](../principles/secondary-action.md): blinks, shadows, dust after landings.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Shape morphing](../techniques/morphing.md): star-to-badge and corner morphs.
- [Faux 3D in a 2D tool](../techniques/faux-3d.md): extruded, chunky shapes.

## Where it is used

- [Explainer videos](../genres/explainers.md)
- [Logo animation](../genres/logo-animation.md)

## Roots

- [Memphis design](../history/memphis-design.md)

## Watch out

- Position keys alone give straight paths: arcs need a pivot or carrier null.
- Overshoot 20–30% only on the playful hero, not on text being read.
- Keep the anchor on the contact edge for squash.
