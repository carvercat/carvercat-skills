# Kinetic typography

A hub page: what the kinetic typography style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Words are the image. Bold weights, high contrast, motion that mirrors meaning, cut tightly to music or voice.

## Principles that carry it

- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): words on beats; vary density.
- [Exaggeration and overshoot](../principles/exaggeration.md): slams with 4–16% overshoot on the hero word.
- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): fast entrances, still holds for reading.
- [Anticipation](../principles/anticipation.md): a beat of stillness before the punchline.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Kinetic typography](../techniques/kinetic-type.md): the full technique page.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): letters 1–2 f, words 2–4 f, lines 4–8 f.
- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): words rising from behind rules.
- [Rhythm editing (cutting to music)](../techniques/rhythm-editing.md): cards cut on beats.

## Watch out

- Reading time first: lint `reading_time` must be clean.
- Scale entrances start at 94% or larger, or lint flags text as too small mid-move.
- Beat-strobing colour changes risk photosensitivity findings.
