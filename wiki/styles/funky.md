# Funky

A hub page: what the funky style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Playful, colourful and a bit rebellious. Memphis shapes, tilts, wiggles, hard offset shadows and stickers.

## Principles that carry it

- [Exaggeration and overshoot](../principles/exaggeration.md): tilts and pops with 15–30% overshoot.
- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): patterns and stickers on the beat.
- [Appeal](../principles/appeal.md): clashing on purpose, never randomly.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Faux 3D in a 2D tool](../techniques/faux-3d.md): hard offset shadows and extrusions.
- [Seamless loops](../techniques/looping.md): slow one-way rotations of decorations.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): sticker stacks and shape bursts.

## Where it is used

- [Social ads and short-form](../genres/social-ads.md)
- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)

## Roots

- [Memphis design](../history/memphis-design.md)
- [Broadcast graphics of the 1980s and 1990s](../history/broadcast-graphics-80s-90s.md)

## Watch out

- Text on a plate: no pattern under words.
- Spin one way, never ping-pong.
- Limit the palette to four to six colours.
