# Minimal luxury

A hub page: what the minimal luxury style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Slow, quiet and precise. Wide letter spacing, warm darks, gold or cream accents, long fades and much empty space.

## Principles that carry it

- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 36–60 f entrances, equal or longer exits.
- [Slow in and slow out (easing)](../principles/slow-in-slow-out.md): S-curves and gentle decelerations, never bounce.
- [Weight](../principles/weight.md): heavy, smooth moves.
- [Appeal](../principles/appeal.md): restraint as personality.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): feathered wipes.
- [Kinetic typography](../techniques/kinetic-type.md): letter spacing that breathes during holds.
- [Camera-like moves in 2D](../techniques/camera-moves-2d.md): very slow drifts and pushes.

## Where it is used

- [Product launch films](../genres/product-launches.md)
- [Title sequences](../genres/title-sequences.md)
- [Logo animation](../genres/logo-animation.md)

## Roots

- [Swiss International Typographic Style](../history/swiss-international-style.md)
- [Mid-century film title design](../history/mid-century-title-design.md)

## Watch out

- Slow fades eat reading time: count from full opacity.
- Wide `letterSpacing` is expensive in width; measure before placing.
- qc reads dark, slow luxury films as black and frozen; judge with `carvercat_look`.
