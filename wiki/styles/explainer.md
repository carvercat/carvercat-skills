# Explainer

A hub page: what the explainer style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Friendly, clear and paced to the voice. Steps, icons, pointers and one emphasis at a time.

## Principles that carry it

- [Staging](../principles/staging.md): one idea per shot.
- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): reading-order stagger at narration speed.
- [Secondary action](../principles/secondary-action.md): one emphasis pulse at a time.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Line drawing and trim paths](../techniques/line-drawing.md): arrows and connectors.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): steps and icon grids.
- [Match cuts and graphic matches](../techniques/match-cuts.md): one object carried between steps.

## Where it is used

- [Explainer videos](../genres/explainers.md)
- [Animated infographics](../genres/infographics.md)
- [UI and UX demo videos](../genres/ui-ux-demos.md)

## Roots

- [Flat design and UI motion](../history/flat-design-and-ui-motion.md)

## Watch out

- Visuals change with the narration, never ahead of it.
- Keywords on screen, not whole sentences.
- Steps share one layout and one entrance.
