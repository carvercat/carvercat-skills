# Retro

A hub page: what the retro style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Nostalgic and warm: 70s sunbursts and stripes, 80s neon and synthwave grids, everything stepped on twos.

## Principles that carry it

- [Straight ahead and pose to pose](../principles/straight-ahead-and-pose-to-pose.md): animation on twos with Hold keys.
- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 12–16 f entrances.
- [Appeal](../principles/appeal.md): imperfection as charm.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Seamless loops](../techniques/looping.md): scrolling grids and suns.
- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): venetian blinds and checker wipes.
- [Faux 3D in a 2D tool](../techniques/faux-3d.md): chrome and extruded type.

## Where it is used

- [Title sequences](../genres/title-sequences.md)
- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)
- [Social ads and short-form](../genres/social-ads.md)

## Roots

- [Broadcast graphics of the 1980s and 1990s](../history/broadcast-graphics-80s-90s.md)
- [Memphis design](../history/memphis-design.md)
- [Mid-century film title design](../history/mid-century-title-design.md)

## Watch out

- Stepped motion on UI-like parts reads as lag; keep it for the retro elements.
- VHS and scanline overlays: avoid full-frame flicker.
- Neon on black: check text contrast with lint.
