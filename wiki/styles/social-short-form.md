# Social short-form (9:16)

A hub page: what the social short-form (9:16) style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Fast, native and loud enough to stop the scroll. Big captions, quick cuts, hooks in the first second.

## Principles that carry it

- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 8–10 f entrances, cuts every 1.5–2.5 s.
- [Staging](../principles/staging.md): one idea per card, frame 0 readable.
- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): cut to the track.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Kinetic typography](../techniques/kinetic-type.md): word-by-word captions.
- [Rhythm editing (cutting to music)](../techniques/rhythm-editing.md): scene changes on beats.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): caption words 3 f apart.

## Where it is used

- [Social ads and short-form](../genres/social-ads.md)
- [Lyric videos](../genres/lyric-videos.md)

## Roots

- [A short history of kinetic typography](../history/kinetic-typography-history.md)

## Watch out

- Lint does not know platform zones: validate text against the 9:16 box yourself.
- Body text 40 px or more on a 1080-wide frame.
- Captions must work sound-off.
