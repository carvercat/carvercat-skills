# Corporate and brand

A hub page: what the corporate and brand style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Trustworthy and composed. Grids, generous space, no springs, soft rises and fades, one accent colour.

## Principles that carry it

- [Slow in and slow out (easing)](../principles/slow-in-slow-out.md): standard and decelerate curves, no overshoot.
- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 20–24 f entrances, 16 f exits.
- [Staging](../principles/staging.md): one message per card.
- [Weight](../principles/weight.md): calm, medium-weight moves.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Line drawing and trim paths](../techniques/line-drawing.md): rules and frames drawn on.
- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): titles revealed by split rules.
- [Camera-like moves in 2D](../techniques/camera-moves-2d.md): slow photo pushes.

- [Broadcast design packages](../genres/broadcast-packages.md)
- [Logo animation](../genres/logo-animation.md)
- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)

## Roots

- [Swiss International Typographic Style](../history/swiss-international-style.md)

## Watch out

- Long holds still need life: a 2–6% push over seconds.
- Lower thirds must survive the longest name.
- Brand colours: convert hex with BT.709 and check a rendered frame.
