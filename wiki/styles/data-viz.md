# Data visualisation

A hub page: what the data visualisation style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Truthful, calm and precise. Motion reveals data in reading order and connects one finding to the next.

## Principles that carry it

- [Staging](../principles/staging.md): one finding per scene, one highlighted series.
- [Slow in and slow out (easing)](../principles/slow-in-slow-out.md): bars and lines arrive decelerating.
- [Exaggeration and overshoot](../principles/exaggeration.md): little or no overshoot: values must not lie.
- [Rhythm](../principles/rhythm.md): bars 3–4 f apart, values after their bar.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Line drawing and trim paths](../techniques/line-drawing.md): lines and timelines that draw themselves.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): bars, rows and segments.
- [Masking and reveals](../techniques/masking.md): area charts revealed by a window.

## Where it is used

- [Data stories](../genres/data-stories.md)
- [Animated infographics](../genres/infographics.md)
- [Explainer videos](../genres/explainers.md)

## Roots

- [Swiss International Typographic Style](../history/swiss-international-style.md)

## Watch out

- Bars grow from a zero baseline (anchor on the bottom edge).
- Axis numbers 28 px or more to pass lint.
- Never rescale an axis without showing it.
