# Futuristic HUD

A hub page: what the futuristic hud style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Precise, technical and alive. Thin lines, brackets, readouts and scans on near-black, cyan with an amber accent.

## Principles that carry it

- [Timing and spacing](../principles/timing-and-spacing.md): 10–14 f entrances, stepped flickers.
- [Straight ahead and pose to pose](../principles/straight-ahead-and-pose-to-pose.md): Hold keys for flicker and stepping.
- [Secondary action](../principles/secondary-action.md): scan lines and ticks around the hero.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Line drawing and trim paths](../techniques/line-drawing.md): brackets and reticles drawn on.
- [Seamless loops](../techniques/looping.md): radar sweeps and rotating rings.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): readout lines typing in.

## Where it is used

- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)
- [UI and UX demo videos](../genres/ui-ux-demos.md)

## Roots

- [Visual music and abstract animation](../history/visual-music-and-abstract-animation.md)
- [Broadcast graphics of the 1980s and 1990s](../history/broadcast-graphics-80s-90s.md)

## Watch out

- Thin lines shimmer when scaled; keep strokes 2–4 px and on whole pixels where possible.
- Flicker stays small and short: qc flags flicker over 3 a second over a quarter of the frame.
- Glow amount 0.8 or less.
