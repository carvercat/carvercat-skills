# Sports

A hub page: what the sports style is, which wiki pages matter most for it, and where its verified Carvercat pack and recipes are.

## The feel

Fast, aggressive and clear. Slashes, impact slams, team colours, big numbers and live-readable score graphics.

## Principles that carry it

- [Anticipation](../principles/anticipation.md): wind-ups before impacts.
- [Weight](../principles/weight.md): heavy slams with a short settle.
- [Exaggeration and overshoot](../principles/exaggeration.md): 6–7% past the impact.

## Techniques it relies on

- [Rhythm editing (cutting to music)](../techniques/rhythm-editing.md): cuts on hits.
- [Camera-like moves in 2D](../techniques/camera-moves-2d.md): zoom-past and shake on impact.
- [Stagger systems](../techniques/stagger-systems.md): stats rows and lineups.

## Where it is used

- [Broadcast design packages](../genres/broadcast-packages.md)
- [Social ads and short-form](../genres/social-ads.md)
- [Event openers and stage screens](../genres/event-openers.md)

## Roots

- [Broadcast graphics of the 1980s and 1990s](../history/broadcast-graphics-80s-90s.md)

## Watch out

- Score bugs and straps must read in under 2 s.
- Shake and flashes on goals: stay within flash limits.
- Test straps with the longest player and team names.
