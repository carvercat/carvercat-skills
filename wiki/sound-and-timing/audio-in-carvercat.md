# Audio clips, levels and fades (measured)

Sound carries half of a motion piece. This page records how Carvercat itself places, levels, fades and mixes
audio clips, measured by exporting audio and reading the samples back, so you can trust frame-accurate sync
and plan levels before the final mix.

## The idea

- An audio clip starts at a frame like any clip; its picture-to-sound sync is exact to that frame.
- Level is set in decibels per clip and can be keyframed for fades and ducking.
- Clips on different audio tracks add together; nothing limits the sum, so the mix can clip.
- Final loudness targets belong to the delivery spec ([delivery](../workflow/delivery.md)); Carvercat gives
  you levels, not loudness metering.

## Rules of thumb

- Keep each source peaking around −6 to −12 dBFS before mixing; leave headroom for the sum.
- Music under voice: duck 8–12 dB while the voice speaks.
- Fades: SFX tails 2–6 f, music in and out 0.5–2 s; avoid fades that are linear in dB if they must sound
  even (see below).
- Place SFX on the impact frame of the picture ([SFX spotting](sfx-spotting.md)).

## Measured in Carvercat

Test sounds were generated (a 1 kHz click on every beat at 120 BPM; a 440 Hz tone at 0.5 peak), placed as
audio clips, exported with `export.set {format:"audio", audioCodec:"wav"}` and read sample by sample:

| Test | Setting | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Sync | click clip starting at frame 15 (30 fps) | first click at **0.500 s** in the export, then every 0.500 s exactly |
| Level | `media.audio` `Level (dB)` −6 | RMS 0.1772 vs 0.3536 source: **−6.0 dB**, peak 0.251 |
| Fade | level keys −60 dB at f0 → 0 dB at f30, 0 dB at f90 → −60 dB at f119 (`linear`) | RMS at 0.25 s 0.0025, 0.5 s 0.0139, 0.75 s 0.0775, 1.0 s 0.346 (full); fade-out mirrored from 3.0 s |
| Mix | the same tone on two tracks, both 0 dB | peak **1.000** (0.5 + 0.5), RMS 0.707: plain addition |
| Format | WAV export | 48 kHz, stereo, 24-bit, exactly the comp length (4.000 s) |

- **Fades are linear in decibels.** Halfway through the 1 s fade-in the level was still about −28 dB below
  full; most of the audible rise happens in the last third. For a fade that sounds even, key an intermediate
  value (for example −60 → −20 dB over the first 20% of the fade, then to 0), or start from −30 dB rather than −60.
- **Sums are not limited.** Two sources peaking at half scale filled the full scale; a third would clip. Turn
  sources down before stacking them.
- `inspect lint` reported no findings on these audio-only comps: it does not check levels or clipping; check
  levels with an export and `ai/scripts/motion/mix_check.py`.

## Do and don't

- Do place each sound by frame and trust the sync.
- Do leave headroom per track; check the export's peak.
- Don't rely on dB-linear fades for musical fades; shape them with an extra key.
- Don't expect lint or qc to warn about audio levels.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every SFX starts on its picture event's frame.
- [ ] Each track peaks below about −6 dBFS; the export peak stays below 0 dBFS.
- [ ] Music fades shaped (not a straight dB ramp).
- [ ] Export format matches the spec (sample rate, bit depth, channels).

## In Carvercat

- Audio clip: a `media` clip on an audio track with `media.audio` `Level (dB)` (−60 to 24, keyframeable); in a
  project file it needs an entry in the project media pool ([JSON authoring](../../ai/references/app/json-authoring.md)).
- Audio-only export: `export.set {settings:{format:"audio", audioCodec:"wav", audioRate:48000, stereo:true, depth:"24"}}`;
  video with sound: `format:"video"` with `sound:true` ([delivery](../workflow/delivery.md)).
- Tools: `sfx_kit.py` / `sfx_render.py` (synthesised SFX), `music_bed.py`, `mix_check.py` (stem loudness) in
  `ai/scripts/motion/`; targets and levels: [sound design](../../ai/references/craft/sound-design.md).
- Lab: comps `A Placement`, `A Gain`, `A Fade`, `A Mix` in [the audio lab](../labs/README.md) (the two test
  WAVs are generated by the lab notes, not shipped).
