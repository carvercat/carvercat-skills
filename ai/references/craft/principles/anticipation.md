# Anticipation

**What it does:** a small move in the opposite direction before the main action tells the eye where to look and makes the action feel powered.

## How in Carvercat
- Key the wind-up as its own pose before the action, and give the action the accelerating ease (a key's `ease` shapes the segment that **leaves** it, so put `ease-in` on the wind-up key; [../motion-principles.md](../motion-principles.md)).
- Ready-made: **Anticipate jump out** (Cartoon Bounce) and **Exit shrink - anticipate** (Motion Patterns).

## Measured examples
| Template | Wind-up | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Anticipate jump out ([cartoon-bounce.md](../../../patterns/cartoon-bounce.md)) | crouch to 118 × 78% over the first 35% (**7 f**) | stretch 82 × 130% and launch up 60% H (stored value), **13 f**, accelerating |
| Character hop ([ct-character-hop.md](../../../patterns/ct-character-hop.md)) | crouch 115 × 80%, f10–18 (8 f) | stretch and launch, f18–22, then the jump |
| Ribbon lower third exit ([ct-ribbon-lower-third.md](../../../patterns/ct-ribbon-lower-third.md)) | the same jump-out on a lower third | gone at the clip end |
| Quiz question ([ex-quiz.md](../../../patterns/ex-quiz.md)) | 50 f of "think time" before the answer | the reveal pulses once (settles f116) |

## Dose
- Wind-up about a third of the action's length; 10–25% of its distance.
- Hero beats only. Utility moves (cards, captions) skip it.
- The quiz shows anticipation in timing, not just in shape: a held pause before a reveal.
