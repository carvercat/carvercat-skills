# Noise bars overlay

**Template** `gl_noise_bars` · Glitch pack · **verified 0.1.6 (rev 1105,; pack added to the bundled Library for the test)**, comp `GL Noise bars`.

A transparent overlay. Six thin bars (6–30 px tall, white, cyan or red) blink on three times each for 2 f at 15–35% opacity, jumping sideways, while a 46 px tracking bar at 6% opacity rolls up the frame.

Lint 0/0; qc found no flashes, including when stacked over the showcase. Place it on the top track over any shot. Keep it below 40% opacity and avoid stacking two copies.
